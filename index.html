The complex, split · the d shell in an octahedral field
Field strength Delta = 0.50 P
Levels are drawn to scale in units of the pairing energy P: t2g at minus 2/5 Delta, eg at plus 3/5 Delta, barycenter zero, all of it integrated out of the true six-charge Coulomb potential by the engine (ratio minus 3/2 to 4e-6; the tetrahedron inverts with Delta_t/Delta_o = minus 4/9 to 3.3e-6). The crossover sits exactly at Delta = P in this model. Orbital moments are quenched as a theorem here: Lz in the real d basis is imaginary antisymmetric, the eg block is identically zero, and the t2g block carries eigenvalues minus 1, 0, plus 1, the t2g-to-p isomorphism. Why 4f escapes all this is radial, and the corpus proves it on its own converged gadolinium: mean radius 0.885 bohr for 4f, buried inside 5p at 1.650 and 6s at 5.037, so the lattice barely reaches it. Chi(T) is the Curie law with CODATA constants for the spin-only moment shown.
